# Customer Retention Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook defines the general retention strategy for customers identified as at risk of churn.

## Risk Tiers

### High Risk

High-risk customers should receive prioritized intervention.

Guidelines:
- Use customer-specific behavioral evidence when selecting an intervention.
- Prioritize the customer's primary behavioral risk driver.
- Consider secondary risk signals when they materially affect the intervention.
- Avoid generic mass messaging when a clear behavioral driver exists.
- Customers with high predicted risk but no strong behavioral driver should be sent for manual review.

### Medium Risk

Medium-risk customers should receive lower-cost proactive engagement.

Recommended approaches:
- Personalized content recommendations.
- Learning progress reminders.
- Light-touch engagement campaigns.
- Continued behavioral monitoring.

### Low Risk

Low-risk customers generally do not require targeted retention intervention.

Recommended approaches:
- Continue standard customer engagement.
- Avoid unnecessary incentives or aggressive outreach.

## Intervention Principles

Retention actions should:
1. Address the identified customer problem.
2. Use recent behavioral evidence.
3. Avoid unnecessary discounts.
4. Avoid making promises that are not supported by company policy.
5. Prioritize service recovery when unresolved service problems exist.
