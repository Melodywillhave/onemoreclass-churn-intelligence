# Engagement Retention Playbook

## Engagement Decline

Engagement decline occurs when a customer's recent learning behavior has materially decreased compared with their previous activity.

Relevant signals may include:
- Declining login activity.
- Declining minutes watched.
- Declining assignment activity.
- Declining active learning days.

### Recommended Actions

Use personalized re-engagement outreach.

Possible interventions:
- Remind the customer about unfinished courses.
- Highlight progress already made.
- Recommend continuing recently viewed content.
- Suggest a small and achievable next learning activity.
- Provide personalized course recommendations based on recent learning behavior.

### Messaging Guidance

Messages should:
- Reference relevant learning activity when appropriate.
- Focus on helping the learner resume progress.
- Keep the next action simple.
- Avoid overly frequent notifications.

## Low Recent Engagement

Low recent engagement means the customer is still using the platform but has relatively low current activity.

### Recommended Actions

- Recommend relevant courses.
- Refresh the customer's learning plan.
- Highlight short or easy-to-resume learning content.
- Recommend a manageable next learning goal.
- Use low-pressure engagement messaging.

### Avoid

- Aggressive win-back messaging.
- Immediate discounting without evidence of price sensitivity.
- Treating low engagement as a service problem unless service-friction signals are also present.
