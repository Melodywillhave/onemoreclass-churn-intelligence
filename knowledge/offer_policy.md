# Retention Offer Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines when incentives or promotional offers may be considered as part of customer retention.

## General Policy

Discounts should not be the default response to churn risk.

An offer may be considered when:
- There is evidence of price sensitivity.
- The customer has meaningful purchase history.
- The customer is eligible for the offer.
- The offer supports the identified retention objective.

## Appropriate Uses

Potential offer scenarios include:
- A previously paying customer showing price-sensitive behavior.
- A customer with repeated discount usage and declining purchase activity.
- An eligible customer considering renewal or another course purchase.

## Inappropriate Uses

Do not automatically offer discounts when:
- The primary problem is unresolved service friction.
- The customer has little or no purchase history.
- The primary problem is learning engagement rather than price.
- No evidence of price sensitivity exists.

## Restrictions

The retention system must not:
- Invent promotions.
- Promise unauthorized refunds.
- Promise credits or compensation.
- Guarantee eligibility for an offer.
- Generate an offer amount that is not defined by company policy.

When offer eligibility is uncertain, recommend manual review.
